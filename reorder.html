How is this calculated?
  • Stock is the live inventory. Open POs are Yooz orders not received yet.
  • It takes away what today's production plan needs, day by day, and after the plan ends what we usually use (what was received in the last 30 days).
  • Lead time = the days in which 9 out of 10 orders from that supplier arrived in the last 12 months (from Yooz).
  • Order by = the last day to place the PO so it arrives with 5 days to spare. About = enough for 2 weeks after it arrives.
  • It is a check, not a PO: the official PO is still made in Yooz.
